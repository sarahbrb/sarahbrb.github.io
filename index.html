Sarah BOUARABA

PhD Fellow in Computer Science · LIG & MIAI Cluster, Université Grenoble Alpes

I am Sarah Bouaraba, a researcher working at the intersection of natural language processing, information retrieval, and healthcare. I believe AI should serve people, and in medicine, where the stakes are human, I want to build models that are thoughtful rather than just powerful. I am currently a Ph.D. student at the LIG Lab (MRIM and GETALP teams) in Grenoble, within the AI & Language Chair of the MIAI Cluster, supervised by Prof. Lorraine Goeuriot, Prof. Didier Schwab, and Prof. Philippe Mulhem. I study how clinical large language models can better retrieve and use medical information, with a focus on synthetic data generation, retrieval-augmented generation, and multilingual, low-resource settings.
